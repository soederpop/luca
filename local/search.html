Semantic search over luca describe

Every feature, client, server, and method luca ships, embedded in your browser. Type what you want to do, not what it is called.

Results

Index the corpus first (one-time embedding of every entry), then search. Searching re-embeds only the query.

What happens

describe-index.json is generated from the same introspection data that powers luca describe. embeddings.embed(texts) runs them through MiniLM in batches; vectors stay in memory. A search is one more embed() plus a dot product per entry, so it is instant after indexing.
